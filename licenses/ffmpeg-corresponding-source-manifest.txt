FFmpeg corresponding-source audit for Stash Video

Binary audited: FFmpeg 9.0.1 from the Martin Riedl macOS release build,
downloaded from the architecture-specific URLs in
Scripts/helper-binaries.lock.json. The arm64 and x86_64 binaries report the
same configure options; both are statically linked builds.

Configure options reported by `ffmpeg -buildconf`:
--enable-gray --enable-libxml2 --enable-version3 --enable-gpl --enable-openssl
--enable-libfreetype --enable-fontconfig --enable-libharfbuzz
--enable-libbluray --enable-libsnappy --enable-libsrt --enable-libvmaf
--enable-libass --enable-libklvanc --enable-libzimg --enable-libzvbi
--enable-libaom --enable-libdav1d --enable-libopenh264 --enable-libopenjpeg
--enable-librav1e --enable-libsvtav1 --enable-libvpx --enable-libvvenc
--enable-libwebp --enable-libx264 --enable-libx265 --enable-libmp3lame
--enable-libopus --enable-libvorbis --enable-libtheora

The corresponding build instructions are archived in
ffmpeg-martin-riedl-build-script.tar.gz. SHA-256:
025ae571e47d09bc7fe74fceae7f7b0b3b73bf304849133e24d5c4581e592473

Publisher source repository:
https://git.martin-riedl.de/ffmpeg/build-script

The archive's DEPENDENCY.md identifies the dependency graph. The source
projects used by this build include FFmpeg, libxml2, OpenSSL, FreeType,
fontconfig, FriBidi, HarfBuzz, libbluray, snappy, SRT, libvmaf, libass,
libklvanc, zlib, zimg, zvbi, AOM, dav1d, OpenH264, OpenJPEG, rav1e, SVT-AV1,
libvpx, vvenc, libwebp, x264, x265, LAME, Opus, Vorbis, Theora, and libogg.
Their upstream URLs and license terms are maintained by the publisher's build
script and its dependency metadata. This manifest is an audit record, not a
replacement for the individual source archives where a component license
requires source to accompany redistribution.

Audit limitation: the publisher's prebuilt archive does not expose every
dependency commit or source archive inside the FFmpeg zip. Before a release,
verify the publisher's source offer and component-specific redistribution terms
for the exact build, or build the binary from the archived script and pinned
sources.
